Predict the Future of Clean Energy

This website is a renewable energy forecasting tool. You enter local weather data such as temperature, wind speed, humidity, and solar irradiance, and the app uses a trained machine-learning model to estimate how much power a solar or wind system can generate. It helps students, engineers, and planners quickly see how changes in weather can increase or decrease clean energy production.
